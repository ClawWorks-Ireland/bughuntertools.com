## The Story

Every bug bounty hunter knows the feeling: you've found something real, the writeup is done, and then you hit the submission form — and it eats your report, rejects your severity, or pends in triage for weeks because you picked the wrong vulnerability category.

Platforms are not interchangeable. Intigriti uses a CORS-specific dropdown. HackerOne builds the CVSS calculator into the form. Bugcrowd has the VRT taxonomy that you need to know cold or your P2 ends up triaged as P4. YesWeHack is the EU-native platform most researchers overlook until a big fintech program shows up there exclusively.

This article is a field guide to submitting correctly — the first time — on all four.

---

## Intigriti (Eu-First, Researcher-Friendly)

- **Submission URL pattern:** `https://app.intigriti.com/researcher/submit/{company}/{program}`
- **Key differentiator:** Programs require `X-Intigriti-User` header during testing — if you didn't send it, you may have violated RoE even if your finding is valid
- **Severity caveat:** CVSS 7.5 doesn't automatically get you "High" payout — Intigriti triagers re-rate independently; justify your rating in the description
- **Practical tip:** Draft your SUBMISSION.md before opening the form. The Intigriti form doesn't autosave well

## Hackerone (Us Platform, Enterprise Programs)

- **Submission URL pattern:** `https://hackerone.com/reports/new?type=team&report_type=vulnerability&user_name={handle}`
- **Key differentiator:** Built-in CVSS 3.1 calculator — use it; manually-typed CVSS vectors in the description without using the calculator get ignored
- **Severity caveat:** The **Impact** section is weighted heavily. Vague impact = "Informative" even with a valid finding
- **Practical tip:** HackerOne shows if a report exists for the same asset in the last 30 days — if a duplicate warning appears, read it before deciding whether to submit

## Bugcrowd (Vrt Taxonomy Is Everything)

- **Submission URL pattern:** `https://bugcrowd.com/{program_slug}/report`
- **Key differentiator:** VRT (Vulnerability Rating Taxonomy) — a hierarchical tree. Wrong category = wrong severity = lost payout. Invest 2 minutes in getting the VRT path right
- **Severity caveat:** Bugcrowd uses P1–P5 (Priority), not the CVSS bands other platforms use. P3 Medium ≠ $0 on most programs
- **Practical tip:** Check whether the program is "Points Only" vs "Cash" before spending time on a writeup

## Yeswehack (Eu Focus, Telco/Fintech Stronghold)

- **Submission URL pattern:** `https://yeswehack.com/programs/{program_slug}/report-vulnerability`
- **Key differentiator:** EU platform with programs not available anywhere else (French telcos, EU financial services, some retail). If a target is in the EU and you can't find it on Intigriti or H1, check YesWeHack
- **Severity caveat:** CVSS is required (unlike H1 where the calculator auto-computes it). Pre-compute before opening the form
- **Practical tip:** Some YesWeHack programs have French-speaking triagers — write extra-clearly in English. Short, numbered reproduction steps are highly valued
